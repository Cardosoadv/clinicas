# Davinci Feature Backlog 🎨

## Core Features to Expand

### 1. Post-Care Follow-up (Pós-consulta)
- **Description:** Automate or facilitate the sending of WhatsApp messages to tutors 24-48 hours after a procedure/appointment to check on the pet's recovery.
- **Value:** Increases customer loyalty, improves perceived care quality, and allows early detection of complications.

### 2. Low Stock Alerts & Dashboard [COMPLETED]
- **Description:** Implement a visual alert in the dashboard and/or a notification system when a product reaches its `estoque_minimo`.
- **Value:** Prevents stockouts of critical supplies and improves inventory management efficiency.

### 3. Pet Birthday CRM [COMPLETED]
- **Description:** A dedicated view or automated message generator for pets celebrating their birthday.
- **Value:** Strengthens the bond between the clinic and the tutor, driving engagement.

### 4. Health Reminders (Vaccines/Deworming) [COMPLETED]
- **Description:** A module to track and remind tutors about upcoming vaccines or deworming based on previous records.
- **Value:** Recurring revenue for the clinic and better health outcomes for the pets.

### 5. Service-Inventory Link (BOM) [IN PROGRESS]
- **Description:** Allow services to be linked to specific inventory items. When a service is billed, the corresponding stock is automatically deducted.
- **Value:** Accurate inventory tracking and reduced manual entry.

### 6. Vaccine & Deworming Tracking Module [COMPLETED]
- **Description:** Transform the simple "Vacinação OK" tag into a structured module that tracks specific vaccine brands, batch numbers, and automatically calculates the next dose date.
- **Value:** Essential for medical safety, tutor compliance, and recurring clinic revenue.

### 7. Weight & Growth History [COMPLETED]
- **Description:** A dedicated field in the clinical record to track weight over time, with a visual chart to monitor growth or health issues (obesity/malnutrition).
- **Value:** Critical for correct dosage and early detection of systemic health changes.

### 8. Veterinary Dosage Calculator [COMPLETED]
- **Description:** A utility that calculates medication dosages based on the pet's current weight and the drug's concentration/protocol.
- **Value:** Reduces calculation errors and speeds up the prescription process.

### 9. Evolution Templates [COMPLETED]
- **Description:** Pre-defined clinical text templates to standardize and speed up the documentation of common procedures.
- **Value:** Improves data consistency and saves veterinarian time.

### 10. Structured Prescription Module [IN PROGRESS]
- **Description:** A system to create prescriptions with structured items (medication, dosage, route, frequency, duration).
- **Value:** Professionalizes the output for tutors, reduces errors, and creates a searchable medication history for the pet.

### 11. Laboratory Integration
- **Description:** A module to track laboratory exam requests and results, integrating them directly into the pet's medical record.
- **Value:** Centralizes clinical information and facilitates clinical evolution monitoring.

### 12. Document Generator
- **Description:** Automated generation of health certificates, surgical consent forms, and other standard veterinary documents.
- **Value:** Saves time, standardizes communication, and improves professional presentation.
